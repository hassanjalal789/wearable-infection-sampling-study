- **Published:** this overview, the [research summary](docs/RESEARCH_SUMMARY.md), [results and limitations](docs/RESULTS_AND_LIMITATIONS.md), the [contribution statement](docs/CONTRIBUTIONS.md), [publication notes](docs/PUBLICATION_NOTES.md), the [evidence index](docs/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md), the [publication manifest](evidence/PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.csv) and ignore rules; the baseline protocol [`prereg-v1.1.md`](docs/prereg-v1.1.md) and amendments [A1](docs/prereg-v1.2-amendment.md), [A2](docs/prereg-v1.2-amendment-A2.md), [A3](docs/prereg-v1.2-amendment-A3.md) and [A4](docs/prereg-v1.2-amendment-A4-no-hardware.md), with the [modelled-energy parameter provenance](docs/modelled_energy_parameter_provenance.md) and the historical [hardware protocol](hardware/measurement_protocol.md); and four publication-stage guides — the [protocol index](docs/PROTOCOL_INDEX.md), the [methods guide](docs/METHODS_GUIDE.md), the [energy scope guide](docs/ENERGY_SCOPE.md) and the [note on the superseded hardware protocol](hardware/README.md).
- **Purpose:** give a reader the research question, the negative primary finding, the rules the executed study actually followed, the energy scope and its limits, the confirmed contributions, and an honest account of what is and is not yet available.
- **Provenance:** the seven archived documents are byte-identical to the versions I preserved during the research process, including their draft markers and reader-addressed passages; I have not retrospectively edited them for publication. The guides, summary, results document, evidence index, manifest, notes and this changelog were written later for this repository and are identified as new writing.
- **Validation:** every archived document compared byte for byte with the version I preserved during the research; the eleven derived energy parameters compared against the frozen scenario configuration across the LOW, CENTRAL and HIGH scenarios (33 comparisons, all equal); the non-execution of the hardware protocol checked against A4.1, the protocol's own empty replication record and the contents of the preserved hardware folder; reported result values checked against the archived digest, results table, comparison summary, gatekeeping record and run manifest; a privacy scan of every file in the snapshot and of the commit metadata, with each match reviewed and none unresolved; and all relative links checked.
- **Limitations:** amendments A5–A7, the pre-outcome checkpoint, the research timeline, the analysis code, configurations, tests, environment records, result files and reproducibility reports are not yet published. No test was rerun, no figure regenerated, and no analysis re-executed for this publication.
