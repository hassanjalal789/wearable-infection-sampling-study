# Changelog

Publication changes by date (Asia/Karachi). These are publication dates. The research chronology is described in the documentation, not here.

## 2026-09-24 — Modelled energy and sampling implementations

- **Published:** the energy model [`energy_model.py`](src/energy_model.py), the budget-to-burst matching code [`modelled_energy_match.py`](src/modelled_energy_match.py), the frozen scenario configuration [`modelled_energy_scenarios.json`](configs/modelled_energy_scenarios.json), the pre-outcome feasibility audit [`modelled_energy_match_audit.json`](results/modelled_energy_match_audit.json), the schedule engine [`schedules.py`](src/schedules.py), and a new [energy and schedules guide](docs/ENERGY_AND_SCHEDULES.md).
- **Purpose:** let a reader check how the E3 budget became seven ten-minute bursts under every energy scenario, and where each schedule places those bursts.
- **Provenance:** the five archived files are byte-identical to the versions I preserved during the research process; I have not retrospectively edited them for publication. The guide and the additions to the overview, methods guide, evidence index and manifest were written later for this repository.
- **Validation:** all five archived files hashed against the versions I preserved; the feasibility audit recomputed from the published code and configuration, with byte-for-byte identical output; all eleven parameters in each scenario, every burst count and every matching error in the guide checked against the configuration and audit; the E3 start times in the guide produced by calling the published schedule module; privacy scan with every match reviewed.
- **Limitations:** energy is modelled for a reference architecture, not measured; no battery life is reported. S3r and S6 are counterfactual and could not run in real time. The tests that exercise these modules are not yet published.

## 2026-09-20 — Complete methods and research chronology

- **Published:** amendments [A5](docs/prereg-v1.2-amendment-A5-runner-operationalization.md), [A6](docs/prereg-v1.2-amendment-A6-phase2-sparse-step-semantics.md) and [A7](docs/prereg-v1.2-amendment-A7-sensitivity-operationalization.md) and the [pre-outcome checkpoint](docs/PRE_OUTCOME_CHECKPOINT.md); a new [research timeline](docs/RESEARCH_TIMELINE.md); a [timeline fact file](evidence/TIMELINE_FACTS.md) tracing each chronology fact to a preserved record; and [execution excerpts](evidence/EXECUTION_EXCERPTS.md) giving run configuration, the frozen calibration rule and participant-flow counts.
- **Extended:** the [methods guide](docs/METHODS_GUIDE.md) now completes the crosswalk through A7 and adds two sections — one on the sparse-step correction and the superseded run, one on the sensitivity analyses and the planned analyses that were not run. The [protocol index](docs/PROTOCOL_INDEX.md), [evidence index](docs/EVIDENCE_INDEX.md), [publication manifest](evidence/PUBLICATION_MANIFEST.csv) and this overview were updated to match.
- **Purpose:** let a reader follow the amendment chain from the baseline protocol to the executed analysis without gaps, see which documents were written before any outcome was inspected and which came after the primary result, and check the chronology against the records rather than taking it on trust.
- **Provenance:** the four archived documents are byte-identical to the versions I preserved during the research process, including their draft and status markers; I have not retrospectively edited them for publication. The timeline, fact file, execution excerpts and guide extensions were written later for this repository and are identified as new writing.
- **The superseded run.** The analysis ran twice. A quality-control audit of the first execution — carried out before any win/loss/tie count, detection rate, warning time, p-value or effect size was inspected — found implausible input-dependent behaviour traced to the Phase-2 step stream carrying no explicit zero-step rows. Amendment A6 confines the correction to that encoding difference and leaves the cohort, budget, schedules, detector, calibration floor, endpoint and gatekeeping frozen. The superseded run is retained unchanged, is not eligible for confirmatory inference, and none of its outputs appears in any result reported here.
- **Validation:** the four archived documents compared byte for byte against the versions preserved in the research materials; run configuration, the calibration rule record and participant-flow counts transcribed from the preserved records and checked field by field; the two run manifests compared and found to differ only in the Phase-2 step-semantics field; the approved timeline paragraph and the contribution statement confirmed unchanged; a privacy scan of every published file; and all relative links checked.
- **Limitations:** the sensitivity outputs, analysis code, configurations, tests, environment records, result tables and figures remain unpublished. No test was rerun, no figure regenerated and no analysis re-executed for this publication. The chronology rests on document headers and a preserved audit trail that fixes order but carries no wall-clock times; facts that could not be established from a record are listed as unestablished in the [fact file](evidence/TIMELINE_FACTS.md).
